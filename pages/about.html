Our History

Good building starts with good thinking.

Reno–Build Construction Limited is a Ghanaian registered, Limited Liability Company, incorporated under the Companies Code. We are registered with the FAO UK and FAO Ghana at the UN Market Place with registration number 223649.

We at Reno – Build Construction Limited have the conviction that the right professionals with a passion for quality, timely delivery and economy are the right people our industry needs for the future. In line with the above our managerial and technical teams have some of the most talented and experienced professionals with vast experiences in the construction industry.

Based on our client’s needs, Reno-Build Construction Ltd. embraces innovation and other alternative delivery methods to provide the most effective and efficient solutions for each project. We pride ourselves in our commitment to our employees, safety, and our longstanding client relationships built through years of outstanding quality and integrity.

Organizational Structure

Company Structure

Reno-Build Construction Ltd. employs a hands-on philosophy both on the job and in the office. By staffing every project with the right professional, both our firm and our clients can be assured their project is of the utmost importance. All of our projects have the support of the office staff which ranges from Professional Engineers, Quantity Surveyors, scheduling professionals, Architects and more. Specific tasks our past clients have requested from us include:

1. Structural Engineering

2. Project Management

3. Cost Estimation

4. Construction Management

5. Architectural Drawings

6. Structural Engineering Drawings

7. Safety Review
